# Commercial Model

## Commercial proposition

Sell the product as customer-hosted licensed software with implementation, annual maintenance and separately priced enhancements or connectors.

## Revenue components

### Design-partner pilot

Indicative structure:

- One Jira environment
- One portfolio spreadsheet format
- Up to 20 projects
- One update cadence
- One leadership dashboard
- One report template
- Measured pilot outcomes
- Defined duration and support

Pilot fees may be credited partly toward production purchase.

### Production licence

Price based on:

- Legal entity or business unit
- Number of portfolios
- Number of connectors
- Deployment environments
- Security and SSO requirements
- Report templates
- Support level

Avoid unpredictable reader-seat pricing for leadership and contributors.

### Implementation

Charge for:

- Discovery
- Field mapping
- Source-authority configuration
- Cadence and escalation setup
- Identity and connector setup
- Report templates
- UAT and handover
- Deployment assistance

### Annual maintenance and support

Include:

- Security fixes
- Supported connector compatibility
- Minor upgrades
- Deployment images
- Documentation updates
- Defined support allowance

### Additional services

- New connectors
- Customer-specific workflows
- Major modules
- Complex migration
- Major-version upgrade
- Premium SLA
- Source access
- Escrow
- Training and PMO process redesign

## Indicative early ranges

These are planning assumptions, not fixed prices.

| Offer | Indicative range |
|---|---:|
| Design-partner pilot | EUR 5,000 to 10,000 |
| Standard production licence and implementation | EUR 25,000 to 50,000 |
| Enterprise source-access licence | EUR 50,000 to 100,000+ |
| Annual maintenance | 18% to 22% of licence value |

Pricing must be validated through customer conversations and pilot proposals.

## Cost ownership

### Vendor

- Product development
- Demo hosting
- Product support tooling
- Release packaging
- Core maintenance

### Customer production

- Infrastructure
- PostgreSQL service
- Jira and Microsoft licences
- Identity provider
- AI usage
- Email or collaboration licences
- Backup storage
- Customer security operations

## Commercial guardrails

- Do not promise unlimited customisation.
- Do not transfer core IP by default.
- Do not price only as a one-time implementation.
- Do not include unrestricted source rights in the standard licence.
- Do not make unsupported compliance claims.
- Define support boundaries for customer-modified code.
